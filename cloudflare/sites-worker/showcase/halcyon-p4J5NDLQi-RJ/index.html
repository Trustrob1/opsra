Questions people ask.

Is what I say confidential?

Yes. What you share stays between us, apart from the rare cases where safety is at risk. I explain this fully on our first call.

Do you see clients outside Nigeria?

Yes. Sessions run online, and the booking page shows every time in your own time zone. You can pay in naira or dollars.

How much is a session?

An individual session is ₦40,000 or $30. The first 15-minute call is free. Couples and coaching rates are shown when you book.

What if I need to change my time?

You can reschedule up to 24 hours before your session, from the link in your confirmation message.
